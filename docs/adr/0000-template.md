# NNNN. Title that states the choice

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (when the decision was made, not when this record was written)

## Context

The situation that forced a choice, and the constraints that applied. Facts only.

## Decision

What was chosen, in one or two sentences, then any detail needed to apply it.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was seriously weighed, and why it lost.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it rules out.

## Sources

Director log decisions this record absorbs, by ULID. `director show <ulid>` prints each one (`--json` gives the complete record).
